Contracts: deterministic, clearly labeled extractive/template answers; no generative API or pretend LLM. The brief permits this. Semantic queries use real persisted vectors; complete lists/aggregation scan all accepted records. Explicit CLI intent/company/location/title/time controls supplement natural-language patterns. Unsupported/ambiguous requests return an explanation, never a plausible nearest-neighbor answer.

Capture one aware UTC reference instant. Last seven days means a rolling publication window, this month means UTC calendar month to that instant, vague recently means 30 days. Publication is not transaction/event time. Future articles excluded. Time filters are exposed in answers. Financial support/debt/closing status are not silently counted as completed startup equity raises.

Every factual answer item retains exact body evidence with offsets and source URL. Revenue excerpts preserve subject/period/forecast context; profit, valuation and market size are not revenue. Layoff mentions are separated into current announcements, historical, negated/conditional and unresolved timing. Empty results do not establish that no real-world events occurred.

Investor names are normalized conservatively; distinct funds in one group are not merged. Duplicate articles about one company/round must not inflate counts. Missing event identity creates an explicit uncertainty group, not a confident extra round. Extraction investor recall remains limited; a zero repeat result is corpus/extraction-limited.

Final eight sample outputs are generated by the CLI engine and saved to results/sample_queries.md with reference time, URLs and honest per-query judgments. Query correctness is not inferred from source matching alone. Packaging/clean-machine/repository/demo remain a later checkpoint.
